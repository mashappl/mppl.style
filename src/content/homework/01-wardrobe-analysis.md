## The *brief*

Pick three pieces that could carry a whole week of everyday outfits, and explain why they work together.

## My *work*

- **Black wool blazer.** The anchor. Sharp shoulders, easy over a knit or a tee.
- **Black leather loafers.** Polish without trying; work with trousers, denim and skirts.
- **Brown crew-neck knit.** Brings warmth and colour so the black never feels severe.

## *Reflection*

Neutral, but not flat: the brown knit does the heavy lifting.
