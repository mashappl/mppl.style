## The *brief*

Choose one runway look, break down what makes it work, and translate it into pieces a client could actually wear.

## My *work*

- **The contrast.** Black tailoring against a white pleated skirt keeps it graphic.
- **The translation.** A sharp black blazer is the everyday version of the look.

## *Reflection*
