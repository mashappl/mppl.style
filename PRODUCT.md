# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Course tutors and classmates** (now): review homework from Mariya's personal stylist course. They open a specific assignment, look at the work, and read the reasoning.
- **Future clients** (growing): people deciding whether to hire Mariya as a personal stylist. They want to see how she thinks and whether her taste fits them.

## Product Purpose

mppl.style is Mariya Popel's personal site. Today it hosts course homework, one page per assignment. Over time it becomes her stylist portfolio. Success: tutors can review homework easily, and a prospective client leaves trusting her eye and knowing what she offers.

## Positioning

A stylist in training who shows her work in the open, assignment by assignment, so the learning itself is the proof of taste and method.

## Operating Context

- Mariya adds homework herself as Markdown files with photos (mood boards, colour analysis, outfit photos, capsule plans), then pushes to GitHub; GitHub Pages deploys to mppl.style.
- Content is image-heavy; most photos are phone shots or collages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro static site, content collection `homework` (title, module, date, summary, optional cover, draft).
- Bilingual: English and Ukrainian, switchable. Copy for both languages is needed.
- Focus areas: everyday wardrobe and capsules, colour and body analysis, shopping and closet edits.
- Undecided: services, pricing, contact method, Instagram or other socials.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: mppl.style (domain). Person: Mariya Popel.

## Evidence on Hand

- One intro entry ("Starting the course"). No real homework, client work, photos, testimonials or portrait yet. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads: photos and boards are the content; the site frames them.
2. Show the reasoning, not just the result, since that is what tutors grade and clients buy.
3. Adding a homework entry must stay a one-file job for Mariya.
4. Everyday and real-people styling, not runway fantasy.
